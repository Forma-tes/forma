
# Debt card content by type

Debt cards keep the existing shared layout, progress indicator, edit/delete actions, and payment action, while showing type-specific labels and details:
- Installment and Buy Now, Pay Later: purchase amount, total repayment, monthly installment, purchase date, remaining balance, cost, duration, next installment.
- Credit card: card/provider, amount spent, remaining balance, paid so far, due date, purchase/statement date, payment mode, payment account.
- Personal loan: loan amount, total to repay, monthly payment, amount paid, remaining, cost, lender, start date, next payment.
- Other: amount owed, paid, remaining, who it is owed to/from, date recorded, due date, payment account.
- Bank loan: retains loan amount, total repayment, monthly installment, paid/remaining, cost, start date, duration, and next installment.

This is presentation-only; it does not alter saved debt data or the existing card interaction flow.
